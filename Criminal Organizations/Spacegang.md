**SpaceGang** is a loose, decentralized criminal syndicate operating across several star systems, particularly along frontier trade routes and regions with limited security presence. It has no single acknowledged government, corporate structure, or permanent headquarters. Instead, the organization consists of pirate crews, smugglers, thieves, hijackers, fences, and smaller gangs that cooperate when useful.

Individual SpaceGang crews vary considerably in size and organization. Some are little more than armed raiders operating a single vessel, while others maintain ships, safehouses, black-market contacts, and semi-permanent territory. Crews exchange information, equipment, personnel, and access to criminal markets through informal networks rather than a centralized chain of command.

The syndicate is heavily associated with **piracy, cargo theft, hijacking, smuggling, black-market trade, and extortion**. SpaceGang crews frequently target commercial freighters and civilian transports, stealing vessels outright or stripping valuable cargo before abandoning them. Weapons, narcotics, restricted technology, stolen industrial equipment, and falsified documentation are common parts of its trade.

SpaceGang vessels are notorious for using **false transponder codes, stolen registrations, repainted hulls, and improvised identification markings**. There is no standardized uniform, ship class, or equipment set across the organization, and crews often modify civilian or stolen vessels according to whatever equipment is available.

The most widely recognized SpaceGang symbol is a rough **gear-like circle surrounding a red four-pointed star**. Variations of the symbol appear as graffiti, hull markings, container tags, tattoos, and crew insignia. Use of the symbol is inconsistent, however, and many affiliated crews retain their own names and markings.

Known SpaceGang-associated groups include the **Driftworks**, **Red Star Haulers**, **Gear Market**, and **No Fixed Home**, along with numerous temporary salvage crews, dock gangs, and station-based criminal organizations. Association between these groups can be temporary, and a crew considered part of SpaceGang in one region may operate independently elsewhere.

The [[United Colonies Security Authority]] classifies SpaceGang as a significant multi-system criminal threat and advises civilian vessels to avoid engagement with suspected crews. Despite repeated arrests and interdiction operations, the organization's decentralized structure makes it difficult to dismantle as a whole.
