**Lambrianou** is an interstellar water-resource and logistics company specializing in the recovery, refinement, and transportation of water from asteroids and comets. Founded around the **22nd century**, the company is headquartered at [[Ceres Station]] in the Asteroid Belt.

Its operations begin with the identification and extraction of water-rich bodies. Crewed and autonomous recovery systems harvest ice from asteroids and comets, after which the material is processed into purified water suitable for habitation, agriculture, industry, and other large-scale uses.

Lambrianou maintains dedicated refining facilities capable of converting raw extraterrestrial ice into transportable water supplies. These facilities remove contaminants, process material for different industrial standards, and prepare large quantities for shipment aboard specialized tanker vessels.

Bulk water transport forms the core of the company's logistics network. Lambrianou tankers move enormous volumes between extraction sites, orbital settlements, planetary colonies, industrial facilities, and stations that cannot meet their needs through local sources.

The company is particularly important to dry colonies and remote habitats where naturally accessible water is limited. In such regions, Lambrianou can function as an essential utility supplier rather than an ordinary commercial vendor, with entire settlements depending on regular deliveries.

Its operations extend through the inner system and onto interstellar routes, making Lambrianou one of the better-known suppliers of off-world water. The company promotes the use of native space resources as a way to reduce dependence on planetary exports and support settlements in environments that would otherwise be difficult to sustain.
