**Batran Combine** is a large agricultural and food-supply organization operating across inhabited space. Its business spans planetary agriculture, controlled-environment farming, food processing, storage, and distribution, with a particular focus on maintaining reliable food production in colonies and settlements that cannot depend entirely on local growing conditions.

The Combine develops and operates extensive farming systems ranging from conventional planetary fields to enclosed agricultural environments. On developed worlds it manages large mechanized farms, while harsher environments may rely on biodomes and other controlled growing systems capable of producing dependable harvests regardless of local weather or soil conditions.

Batran's operations extend well beyond cultivation. The company processes crops, manages storage infrastructure, and distributes finished food products through regional supply networks. This allows it to provide governments, colonies, private settlements, and commercial partners with complete agricultural systems rather than simply selling raw produce.

Biodome agriculture is a particularly important part of the company's business. Batran constructs and operates enclosed growing environments designed for high-yield production in locations where conventional agriculture would be unreliable or impossible. These installations are commonly used by orbital communities, developing colonies, and settlements with limited arable land.

The company publicly emphasizes stability and food security. Batran contracts often involve long-term supply agreements intended to keep settlements independent of irregular imports or emergency shipments. In some regions, Batran infrastructure becomes part of the basic economic foundation of a colony, supporting both local consumption and export agriculture.

Batran frequently works alongside governments, colonial authorities, and private development groups. Its services overlap with agricultural organizations such as [[Standridge Band]], [[Popotas Society]], and [[Panstellar Multistellar]], though Batran places particular emphasis on large-scale integrated agricultural systems and controlled-environment production.
