- Commonly called **Greys** by humans. "Roswell" became the more formal human name for the species because of their connection to the 1947 Roswell incident.
- A real Roswell craft came down on Earth in 1947. The event was significant in Roswell history as well as human history.
- Humanity spent centuries studying recovered technology before understanding enough of it to reproduce even basic principles.
- Human **jump/relay technology ultimately derives in part from Roswell technology**, although modern human systems are not simple copies.
- Roswells remain technologically ahead of humanity, particularly in areas such as gravity, materials, medicine, genetics, sensors, and neural technology.
- Their resemblance to centuries of human "Grey alien" stories is not coincidence. Some historical UFO encounters may have involved actual Roswells.
- They are playable
  
---
  
Characteristics
Roswell Travellers have the following modifiers applied to their characteristics: STR-1, END-1, INT+1.

Traits
Roswell Travellers all possess the following traits:

Innate Psionics: All Roswells are naturally psionic and possess a PSI characteristic. When determining PSI, roll 3D and keep the highest two dice. Unlike other species, Roswells do not suffer any reduction to PSI based on age or number of terms served.

Limited Visual Tracking: Roswells possess extremely large eyes with limited ability to shift their focus without moving their head. They suffer DM-2 to Recon checks where rapidly scanning or visually tracking their surroundings is important.

Unfamiliar Combat Physiology: Roswells suffer DM-1 to Gun Combat and Melee checks.

Low Light Adaptation: Roswells evolved under extremely low light conditions and can see in low light and darkness without penalty. Their vision has poor colour perception but is exceptionally sensitive to available light.

Light Sensitivity: In conventionally lit environments, a Roswell suffers DM-2 to all checks due to painful glare and visual halos. In extremely bright conditions, the Roswell must make a Difficult (10+) END check or fall unconscious for 1D6 rounds. While conscious in extremely bright conditions, the Roswell suffers DM-4 to all checks and its hearing is impaired.
