**Threat Circle** is a defense technology company specializing in **military sensors, artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, targeting systems, and integrated command infrastructure**. Its products are designed primarily to give military forces a complete picture of an operational environment before and during an engagement.

A major part of the company's business is **multi-spectrum detection and tracking**. Threat Circle manufactures sensor platforms and optical systems capable of monitoring orbital space, planetary approaches, deep-space corridors, and other large operational areas. Data from these systems can be combined into a shared battlespace picture rather than requiring individual vessels or installations to operate from isolated sensor feeds.

Threat Circle's artificial-intelligence systems process this information to classify objects, track movement, identify patterns, assess potential threats, and assist operators in prioritizing targets. These systems are commonly integrated with command-and-control networks, allowing information gathered by numerous ships, stations, drones, and sensor platforms to be processed as a single operational network.

The company also develops **autonomous military platforms**, including unmanned reconnaissance systems, orbital interceptors, and other remotely or independently operated assets. These systems are generally intended to work as extensions of a larger military network rather than as isolated units.

Its **guidance and fire-control systems** connect surveillance information directly to weapon platforms, supporting engagements between spacecraft as well as attacks against planetary or orbital targets. Threat Circle also supplies integrated mission architecture that combines sensors, communications, intelligence processing, autonomous systems, and weapons coordination into a unified command environment.

Because of this focus, Threat Circle equipment is commonly found aboard military vessels, defense stations, surveillance installations, and large command centers where rapidly combining information from many sources is considered more important than any single sensor or weapon system.

Public company material claims more than **120 years of operational experience**, over **50 major deployments**, and systems designed for eight different military operational domains.
