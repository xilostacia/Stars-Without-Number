The **G'naak** are large predatory animals found on a number of inhabited worlds. They are closely related to the [[K'kree]], with both species descending from the same broad ancestral stock. Where the K'kree evolved as large herd-dwelling prey animals, the G'naak developed along a predatory branch. Their shared ancestry remains obvious in their general body structure, but the G'naak are leaner, more flexible, and considerably more heavily adapted for hunting.

## Biology
G'naak are large quadrupeds with a raised forebody and a pair of smaller manipulative forelimbs. Their build allows them to move quickly while still using the upper limbs to seize, hold, or tear at prey. Unlike the K'kree, their sensory organs are directed primarily forward, giving them strong depth perception while stalking or pursuing prey. They possess powerful jaws, prominent teeth, and a lighter frame better suited to sudden bursts of speed and violent close-range attacks. Their coloration varies by population and environment.

## Ecology
G'naak are active carnivores. Their ancestors occupied many of the same environments as the herbivorous ancestors of the K'kree, and the two remain an unusually clear example of closely related animals diverging into predator and prey niches. Wild G'naak generally hunt large herd animals and are dangerous to humans and similarly sized species when hungry, threatened, or accustomed to settlements. They are animals rather than sophonts and display no evidence of sapient intelligence.

## Human Use
G'naak occasionally appear in captivity, particularly in zoological collections, private preserves, research facilities, or on worlds where they are raised for specialized purposes. Unlike the much more commonly domesticated [[K'kree]], they are rarely kept as ordinary livestock.

## Playability
The G'naak are **not available as player characters**.
