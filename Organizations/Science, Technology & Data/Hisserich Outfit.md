**Hisserich Outfit** is a specialist space-weather and radiation-protection company providing forecasting, route analysis, shielding, and emergency response services for spacecraft, stations, habitats, and industrial operations.

The company operates networks of sensors and observatories that monitor stellar activity across heavily traveled regions. These systems track solar flares, coronal mass ejections, radiation fronts, magnetospheric disturbances, and other phenomena capable of threatening ships or exposed infrastructure.

Forecasting is the core of Hisserich's business. Data from its sensor networks is used to predict hazardous stellar events and estimate how radiation conditions are likely to affect nearby routes, stations, and planetary operations. Commercial operators frequently purchase Hisserich data when planning movements through systems with unstable or highly active stars.

Its route-advisory services translate those forecasts into practical navigation guidance. Rather than simply reporting radiation levels, Hisserich models likely exposure along different trajectories and recommends safer departure windows or alternate routes when conditions deteriorate. Its work therefore overlaps somewhat with navigation-intelligence firms such as [[Sine Ring]], though Hisserich is specifically focused on stellar hazards.

Hisserich also manufactures and installs radiation-protection systems. These range from portable shielding arrays and temporary field generators to permanent hardening upgrades for habitats, stations, and industrial facilities. Older installations in hazardous systems are frequently retrofitted with Hisserich equipment rather than replaced outright.

Emergency teams can be deployed when stellar conditions worsen unexpectedly. These units establish temporary shielding, reinforce vulnerable facilities, assist with evacuation or shelter procedures, and provide technical support until radiation levels return to acceptable limits.

Long-haul carriers, orbital facilities, hospitals, industrial habitats, research installations, and security organizations are among the company's major customers. In systems where severe stellar activity is a regular part of life, Hisserich services are often treated as basic operational infrastructure rather than optional insurance.
