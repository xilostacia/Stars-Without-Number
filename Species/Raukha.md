- Large feline extraterrestrial sophonts based mechanically and biologically on Traveller's **Aslan**, but with completely different setting history.
- The Raukha encountered Earth thousands of years before modern human spaceflight.
- A Raukha expedition attempted to establish control over part of ancient Egypt and possessed technology vastly beyond anything humans of the period understood.
- **Bastet was an actual Raukha leader**, rather than the name of the species. Figures remembered as **Mafdet**, and possibly **Mau**, were also connected to the occupation.
- Raukha activity became entangled with Egyptian stories of feline gods and divine beings.
- They were also connected directly to the construction or purpose of the pyramids. The "aliens built the pyramids" story is, inconveniently, substantially true in this setting.
- The occupation ultimately failed. Their expedition was isolated, could not indefinitely replace losses or equipment, and **humans eventually killed the remaining Raukha on Earth**.
- Modern Raukha know what happened. Earth consequently occupies a rather unusual place in their history.
- They are playable.
