**Hsiang Systems** is the defense division of [[Hsiang Enterprises]], producing military and professional weapon systems for armed forces, government agencies, and authorized security organizations. While its civilian parent company focuses on private ownership and sporting markets, Hsiang Systems develops equipment intended for sustained operational use.

Its principal products include assault rifles, duty carbines, optics, fire-control equipment, support systems, and field accessories. Hsiang weapons are built around modular architectures that allow a single platform to be configured for different missions without replacing the entire weapon.

The division's best-known platform is the **S7 modular rifle system**, a configurable family of weapons built around shared components. Barrel assemblies, stocks, handguards, optics, suppressors, and other equipment can be exchanged to adapt the rifle for different environments and operational requirements.

Hsiang also produces military-grade optics and fire-control systems. These range from conventional sights to integrated targeting equipment designed to interface with other battlefield systems. Accessories such as suppressors, magazines, stocks, grips, and support electronics are produced as part of complete weapon packages rather than treated solely as aftermarket equipment.

Interoperability is a major part of the company's defense business. Hsiang Systems markets its products to organizations that need weapons, optics, support equipment, and replacement components to function together across large procurement programs. Standardized components also simplify maintenance and supply for organizations operating across multiple installations or systems.

Hsiang Systems sells through authorized government and defense channels and maintains training, maintenance, and lifecycle-support programs for institutional customers. Its contracts commonly extend beyond the initial sale to include replacement parts, armorer support, upgrades, and continued technical assistance.

The division competes directly with military suppliers such as [[Imani Corporation]], [[Magnus]], and other weapons manufacturers, while remaining closely tied to the civilian engineering base of [[Hsiang Enterprises]].
