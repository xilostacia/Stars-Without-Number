The species humans almost universally call **Vegans** are a highly distinctive extraterrestrial people whose actual species name is **Vh'khlrr-aathei'rral-taikhvarei-vrrakh'tel**. Their native name is difficult for most humans to pronounce correctly and has never seen widespread use outside their own communities. Humans instead derived the name **Vegan** from [[Vega]], the shortened human name for their homeworld. Unfortunately for them, Vegans are also naturally **obligate herbivores**. Humans noticed this almost immediately.

The resulting joke became so widespread that correcting it proved effectively impossible. Modern Vegans generally understand and accept the human term, even if the coincidence responsible for it remains an enduring source of amusement throughout human space.

## Biology
Vegans are deliberately one of the more physically alien playable species encountered by humanity. Their anatomy does not closely resemble the basic human body plan, and many aspects of their physiology make considerably more sense when understood as adaptations for a primarily horizontal posture.

They are tall, lightly built creatures originating from a **lower-gravity environment**. Their limbs are elongated and relatively slender, giving them considerable height when fully raised without the heavy musculature associated with similarly tall humans.

The torso is naturally carried forward rather than vertically. Their feeding and respiratory anatomy developed around this horizontal orientation, including a prominent feeding opening along the torso rather than a conventionally positioned humanoid mouth.

Their unusual head and sensory structures likewise bear little resemblance to those of humans. The result is a species whose appearance remains immediately recognizable even among the increasingly varied populations of interstellar civilization.

## Quadrupedal Movement
A Vegan's normal posture is **quadrupedal**. When travelling normally, all four primary limbs support the body. The forward limbs can alter their shape and position when bearing weight, with their thicker tendril-like digits drawing together and twisting into stable, stump-like supports.

This is not an awkward crawling posture or a temporary adaptation. It is the Vegan body's natural form of terrestrial locomotion.

When fine manipulation is required, a Vegan can raise the front of its body into a tall, hunched bipedal stance. In this position, the forward limbs are freed from locomotion and their several thick tendrils separate for grasping, tool use, operation of machinery, and other precise work.

The transition between the two postures is natural and routine. A Vegan therefore frequently alternates between being a four-legged walking creature and a tall upright worker depending upon what they are doing.

Human imagery sometimes overemphasizes their upright stance because Vegans are most commonly photographed while interacting with equipment or people. In ordinary life, particularly while travelling any significant distance, quadrupedal movement is far more common.

## Manipulation
The ends of a Vegan's forward limbs divide into several **thick flexible tendrils**, generally three or four major digits capable of independent movement.

While standing upright these function as hands, wrapping around controls, tools, weapons, and other objects rather than gripping them with human-style fingers.

When the Vegan returns to quadrupedal movement, those same tendrils draw together and brace against one another, forming the broad temporary foot-like structures used to support the front of the body.

Their rear limbs are more permanently specialized for locomotion and do not possess the same degree of manipulation. This combination allows Vegans to remain effective tool users without sacrificing the quadrupedal body plan from which they evolved.

## Diet
Vegans are **obligate herbivores**. Their digestive system is adapted entirely to plant-derived food, and animal tissue provides little useful nutrition while potentially causing significant digestive problems. This is a biological requirement rather than a cultural dietary philosophy, something humans took approximately no time at all to find funny after beginning to call the species Vegans. Their nutritional requirements can usually be supported easily within human settlements, although Vegan food tends to be prepared differently from human vegetarian cuisine because their biology does not share human nutritional assumptions.

## Gravity
Vegan physiology developed under gravity lower than that of [[Earth]].

Their long limbs and relatively lightweight bodies are well adapted to their native environment but make them less comfortable under substantially heavier gravitational conditions.

Even in Earth-normal gravity they tend to appear unusually light and deliberate in their movements compared with similarly sized humans.

Their quadrupedal posture helps distribute weight across the body, while prolonged upright activity becomes increasingly demanding as gravity increases.

This remains one of the more significant environmental considerations for Vegans travelling outside their own settlements.

## Clothing and Equipment

Human clothing patterns generally fit Vegans poorly.

Their horizontal torso, unusual feeding anatomy, changing posture, and manipulatory forelimbs make conventional shirts, jackets, trousers, and similar garments impractical.

Vegans consequently tend toward **minimal clothing, harnesses, fitted equipment belts, protective coverings, and purpose-built garments** that leave important parts of the torso unobstructed.

This does not indicate a lack of cultural interest in clothing or personal appearance. Their garments simply developed around a completely different anatomy.

Equipment intended specifically for Vegan use similarly accommodates both quadrupedal and upright postures. Controls are often positioned so they can be reached while raised without forcing the Vegan to remain upright longer than necessary.

## Relations with Humanity
Vegans are a recognized part of interstellar civilization and interact regularly with humans. Their appearance remains striking even by the standards of other extraterrestrial species, but they are sufficiently familiar throughout major ports and settled systems that their presence does not normally cause alarm.

The species' willingness to tolerate the human term **Vegan** is representative of a broader practical approach to interacting with humanity. Their actual language, anatomy, and customs remain distinctly their own, but they have generally accepted that human interstellar culture will simplify unfamiliar terminology whenever given the opportunity. The name **Vh'khlrr-aathei'rral-taikhvarei-vrrakh'tel** has therefore survived perfectly well. Humans simply almost never use it.

## Characteristics
Vegan Travellers have the following modifiers applied to their characteristics: EDU+2, SOC-2.

## Traits
Vegan Travellers all possess the following traits:

**Eye Membrane**: The eye membranes of a Vegan provide eye protection sufficient to keep out dust and small particles of grit. Vegan vision extends into the infrared, effectively providing the same capabilities as a set of IR goggles.

**Heat Tolerance**: When encountering conditions hot enough to cause damage, a Vegan can tolerate (and therefore ignore) a number of points of damage equal to their END each hour. Thus most Vegans can operate indefinitely in a very hot desert where a human would suffer 1D damage per hour and can even withstand the heat of a welding torch for a few seconds.

**High Gravity Intolerance**: Vegans double the penalties associated with operating in High Gravity environments and can never acclimatise to it.

**Facultative Quadruped:**(custom) Vegans are naturally quadrupedal and normally move using all four limbs. While moving quadrupedally, their base movement is **9 metres** and they receive **DM+1 to Athletics checks made to maintain balance or resist being knocked prone**. A Vegan may rear into a hunched bipedal stance at any time, reducing its base movement to the normal **6 metres** but freeing its forelimbs for manipulation, tools and weapons. Changing between quadrupedal and bipedal posture does not require an action. A Vegan cannot benefit from its increased quadrupedal movement during a round in which it uses its forelimbs to perform another action.
