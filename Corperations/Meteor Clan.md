**Meteor Clan** is an independent roaming flotilla that functions as a mobile free port, industrial service hub, and trading center. Rather than maintaining a permanent home system, the Clan moves between asteroid belts, remote trade routes, salvage fields, and other regions where conventional port infrastructure is limited or absent.

The flotilla provides docking and cargo handling for independent ships alongside repair facilities, resupply, trade berths, and crew services. Merchants and small operators can conduct business through Meteor Clan without needing access to a major planetary port or corporate station.

Salvage and resource recovery form another major part of its economy. Clan vessels recover derelicts, meteoric material, abandoned cargo, and useful industrial debris, either processing the material themselves or accepting salvage brought in by outside crews. Basic refining facilities allow recovered ore and scrap to be converted into usable commodities before resale.

Meteor Clan's ships and station modules are deliberately modular. Sections of the flotilla can separate, expand, or reorganize depending on local demand, allowing the Clan to operate as a repair yard, market, refinery, resupply point, or salvage base as circumstances require.

The organization describes itself as simultaneously a **clan, crew, and fleet**. It is not aligned with a major government or corporation and is known for dealing with independent operators that larger institutions may overlook. This independence also means that the exact rules aboard the flotilla are largely determined by the Clan itself rather than by whatever government happens to control the nearest inhabited system.

Its operations overlap with salvage companies such as [[Kehl Association]] and resource suppliers such as [[Rimward Extraction]], though Meteor Clan is distinguished by combining those functions with the services of a mobile commercial port.
