The **Roswells**, commonly called **Greys**, are a technologically advanced extraterrestrial species with one of the longest documented relationships with humanity. Although both names are human in origin, neither is considered particularly offensive. "Grey" is ordinary human slang based on their appearance, while "Roswell" became the standard formal human name because of the species' connection to the **Roswell Incident** of 1947.

Roswells possess their own name for their species, but it is rarely used outside their own civilization and has never become important within human society. Roswells themselves generally accept the human terminology. Humanity has become the most widespread and culturally dominant species throughout much of known space, and Roswells tend to treat the use of human names as a practical matter rather than something worth correcting.

## Biology
Roswells are small, lightly built humanoids averaging around **1.3 meters in height**, or a little over four feet. They have disproportionately large heads, slender bodies, hairless skin, long fingers, and enormous dark eyes. Skin coloration varies considerably within shades of grey, ranging from pale grey-blue and silvery tones to much darker matte grey.

Their eyes evolved for extremely low-light conditions and are capable of functioning in environments that would leave humans effectively blind. The tradeoff is significant sensitivity to bright light and relatively poor color perception. Roswell eyes also have limited independent movement compared to human eyes, causing them to turn or tilt their entire head when redirecting their focus.

Roswells are physically less robust than humans. Their musculature and general body structure favor precision and fine manipulation rather than strength or endurance, and they are poorly suited to prolonged physical exertion or close combat.

## Psionics
Every Roswell is naturally psionic. Psionics are not treated as an unusual talent, mutation, or specialized profession within Roswell society. They are simply a normal part of Roswell biology. Individual strength, control, training, and specialization vary, but some level of psionic ability is present throughout the species.

Roswells possess a much deeper understanding of psionics than humanity and have incorporated it into their science, communication, medicine, and technology. Some Roswell devices are partially or entirely controlled through psionic interaction, making them effectively unusable by most humans even when their physical construction can be understood.

This is one of the primary reasons Roswell technology has proven so difficult for humanity to reproduce.

## Technology
Roswell technology is among the most advanced regularly encountered by humanity, sitting only below the highest technological capabilities known to exist.

Their machines are not simply superior versions of human equipment. Roswell civilization developed along an independent technological path, and many of the principles underlying their equipment are incompatible with human engineering traditions.

As a rough measure, humans are capable of meaningfully operating or understanding perhaps **30%** of Roswell technology without extensive adaptation. Roswells, by comparison, can operate roughly **80%** of human technology with relatively little difficulty. Human equipment is generally comprehensible to them, even when they consider its design inefficient or unnecessarily complicated.

These figures represent the broader technological relationship rather than gameplay restrictions. A Roswell living within human society can use ordinary human weapons, computers, vehicles, spacecraft, and other equipment normally.

Roswell spacecraft are particularly advanced. Their ships are capable of independent faster-than-light travel and do not require the relay infrastructure used by humanity. A single Roswell can technically operate one of their vessels, although crews of approximately three are more comfortable for ordinary operations and crews of around five are common for longer missions.

## Early Observation of Earth
Roswells were aware of [[Earth]] long before humanity became an interstellar civilization. For much of that period, humans were simply one of many intelligent but technologically immature species being observed from a distance.

That changed in **1945**. The detonation of the first nuclear weapon during the Trinity test represented a sudden and potentially dangerous advancement in human technology. Roswell observers began paying considerably more attention to Earth, particularly the rapidly developing military and nuclear infrastructure of the United States.

This increased observation directly led to the event that would eventually give the species its human name.

## The Roswell Incident
In **1947**, a small Roswell observation craft crashed near Roswell, New Mexico.

The vessel carried only **two Roswells**, an unusually small crew appropriate for a short surveillance mission. Both died in the crash, and the spacecraft itself was catastrophically damaged. American military personnel recovered the bodies and much of the wreckage before the event could become public knowledge.

The recovered craft resembled what would later become the classic image of the "flying saucer": a relatively simple silver disc whose exterior gave little indication of the complexity of the systems within it.

Human researchers initially understood almost nothing about the wreckage. Components could be examined, dismantled, and catalogued, but most of their functions were unclear and many depended upon technologies humanity had not yet developed.

The incident was publicly concealed, and the recovered material became the foundation of one of the longest-running classified research programs in human history.

## The Return to New Mexico
When the original observation crew failed to report back, the Roswells eventually sent a larger recovery team to determine what had happened.

Their arrival roughly a year later coincided with reports of unusual **green fireballs** and other aerial phenomena over New Mexico. These later visitors were not another observation mission. They were a recovery and salvage team searching for the missing vessel and its crew.

By the time they arrived, the spacecraft had already been removed and divided among American military research programs.

The United States attempted to intercept and repel the returning Roswell craft. These encounters were hostile in the technical sense, but heavily one-sided. Contemporary human aircraft and weapons were incapable of seriously threatening Roswell spacecraft, which could simply evade pursuit.

The Roswells eventually concluded that recovering the original vessel without escalating into a violent confrontation was no longer practical. Their mission changed from recovering the wreckage to observing what humanity intended to do with it.

## Groom Lake and Area 51
Research into the recovered technology eventually became concentrated around [[Groom Lake]].

The publicly famous installation later known as **Area 51** became an extremely effective distraction. Experimental aircraft genuinely operated from the facility, but the mythology surrounding the base was deliberately allowed to grow because it directed public attention toward visible runways, strange aircraft, and dramatic security measures.

The most sensitive Roswell research took place elsewhere within the Groom Lake complex, particularly within heavily secured underground facilities.

By the late 20th century, conspiracy theorists had effectively identified the correct geographical area while misunderstanding most of what was actually taking place there.

[[Groom Lake]] would eventually evolve from a secret research program into the organization responsible for humanity's first practical faster-than-light technology.

## Abductions
Many historical human stories of alien abduction were based on genuine Roswell activity.

Once it became clear that humans possessed their technology, the Roswells began studying humanity much more closely. Individual humans were periodically collected, examined, biologically sampled, mentally observed through psionics, and then returned.

The Roswells generally considered these examinations controlled scientific observation rather than deliberate cruelty. From the perspective of the people involved, however, being paralyzed, removed from one's surroundings, subjected to unfamiliar medical procedures, mentally examined, and later returned with incomplete memories was understandably terrifying.

Classic reports of missing time, unexplained paralysis, fragmented memories, telepathic communication, and dreamlike recollections often originated from Roswell psionic techniques used during these encounters.

Not every historical abduction story was genuine, but enough were that the popular image of the Grey alien remained remarkably close to reality.

## Cattle
Roswells also collected terrestrial animals during their early study of Earth, most famously cattle. Initially, they did not fully understand why humans maintained enormous populations of these animals or the economic and cultural importance placed upon them. Some specimens were examined and returned, while others were not.

Eventually the Roswells discovered the obvious answer. Humans ate them. The Roswells tried beef and found that they liked it.

Living cattle were eventually transported away from Earth and established as livestock within Roswell civilization. By the modern era, domesticated Earth cattle can be found on Roswell worlds and installations, making beef one of the strangest and earliest successful exports from human civilization.

## Humanity's First Faster-Than-Light Travel
For centuries, researchers at [[Groom Lake]] continued studying material recovered from the original Roswell craft.

Humanity never successfully reproduced the Roswell drive itself. Instead, researchers gradually extracted individual principles from technologies they only partially understood and combined them with independently developed human engineering.

The result was crude compared to a Roswell spacecraft, but functional. During the late 21st and early 22nd centuries, Groom Lake finally produced a human spacecraft capable of performing controlled short-range faster-than-light jumps within the [[Sol System]].

The first major demonstration was a crewed jump between [[Earth]] and [[Mars]]. When the human spacecraft arrived, a Roswell vessel was already waiting. The Roswells had observed the program's development and considered the successful jump the point at which continued secrecy no longer served any useful purpose. This became the first official open contact between humanity and an extraterrestrial civilization.

Humanity then learned that the species waiting at Mars was the same species whose ship had crashed in New Mexico more than a century earlier.

## The First Relays
Open contact did not result in the Roswells simply giving humanity their faster-than-light technology. Much of it was biologically or psionically incompatible with humans, and the Roswells were unwilling to hand over technologies humanity could not safely reproduce or fully understand.

Instead, [[Groom Lake]] developed a distinctly human solution: the **FTL relay**. Human ships could not perform unrestricted interstellar jumps. Instead, relay installations established stable routes between star systems. Once a compatible relay existed at each end of a route, human ships could travel between them reliably. This created an immediate problem. Humanity could not build a destination relay without first reaching the destination.

The Roswells solved that problem without surrendering their own drive technology. Early human relay expeditions travelled aboard or alongside Roswell vessels capable of carrying them through interstellar jumps. Human engineers would then construct and activate the destination relay, creating a permanent route back to human space. Once established, the route no longer required Roswell assistance.

These expeditions allowed humanity to begin constructing its first interstellar network while keeping the fundamental distinction between the two civilizations intact. **Roswell ships can travel where they choose. Human ships travel where routes have been built.** That difference continues to shape interstellar politics, trade, exploration, warfare, and settlement into the modern era.

## Modern Roswells
Roswells are now a well-known but relatively uncommon presence throughout human space. Encountering one at a major interstellar port is noteworthy, but no longer extraordinary.

Relations between humanity and Roswell civilization are generally stable. Their shared history remains complicated, particularly regarding the recovery of the original spacecraft, decades of covert observation, human military responses, and the ethics of Roswell abductions.

Roswells nevertheless maintain extensive contact with humanity and are capable of operating comfortably within human settlements and aboard human spacecraft.

Their greater technological sophistication has not translated into political dominance. Humanity's enormous population, territorial expansion, industrial capacity, and relay network have made humans the most widespread cultural presence throughout much of known space.

Roswells largely accept this reality and interact with human civilization on practical terms, even when humans insist on calling them **Roswells** or simply **Greys**.

Characteristics
Roswell Travellers have the following modifiers applied to their characteristics: STR-1, END-1, INT+1.

Traits
Roswell Travellers all possess the following traits:

Innate Psionics: All Roswells are naturally psionic and possess a PSI characteristic. When determining PSI, roll 3D and keep the highest two dice. Unlike other species, Roswells do not suffer any reduction to PSI based on age or number of terms served.

Limited Visual Tracking: Roswells possess extremely large eyes with limited ability to shift their focus without moving their head. They suffer DM-2 to Recon checks where rapidly scanning or visually tracking their surroundings is important.

Unfamiliar Combat Physiology: Roswells suffer DM-1 to Gun Combat and Melee checks.

Low Light Adaptation: Roswells evolved under extremely low light conditions and can see in low light and darkness without penalty. Their vision has poor colour perception but is exceptionally sensitive to available light.

Light Sensitivity: In conventionally lit environments, a Roswell suffers DM-2 to all checks due to painful glare and visual halos. In extremely bright conditions, the Roswell must make a Difficult (10+) END check or fall unconscious for 1D6 rounds. While conscious in extremely bright conditions, the Roswell suffers DM-4 to all checks and its hearing is impaired.
