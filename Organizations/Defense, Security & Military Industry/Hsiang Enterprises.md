**Hsiang Enterprises** is a civilian firearms manufacturer known for precision-built, modular weapons designed for private ownership, sport shooting, professional use, and personal defense. The company produces sidearms, sporting rifles, optics, and a broad catalog of accessories intended for lawful civilian markets across settled space.

Hsiang weapons are recognizable for their clean construction and extensive use of modular components. Many models are designed so barrels, grips, stocks, optics, magazines, and other components can be replaced or reconfigured without requiring an entirely new weapon. The company markets this adaptability as both a practical advantage and a way to keep individual platforms serviceable for long periods.

Sidearms form one of Hsiang's largest product lines, ranging from compact personal-defense weapons to larger duty-pattern pistols. Its sporting rifles cover recreational shooting, hunting, competition, and other civilian applications, with considerable overlap in components across different models.

Hsiang also manufactures its own optics and firearm accessories. Sights, magazines, suppressors, mounting systems, replacement parts, and other equipment are designed to integrate closely with the company's weapons while remaining compatible with common industry standards.

The company places considerable emphasis on responsible ownership, certified dealers, training, and compliance with local regulations. Hsiang firearms are normally distributed through licensed commercial channels rather than direct military procurement networks.

Hsiang Enterprises also maintains a separate defense division, [[Hsiang Systems]], which adapts the company's engineering and modular design philosophy to weapons intended for military, government, and professional security customers. The two organizations share technology and branding but occupy distinctly different markets.

Its civilian weapons compete with products from companies such as [[Imani Corporation]] and [[Magnus]], though Hsiang is particularly associated with highly modular, cleanly engineered civilian platforms.
