**Tsouderos** Casino Fleet operates a small fleet of luxury casino ships that remain almost permanently in transit through recognized free-navigation corridors. The company combines gambling, hospitality, passenger travel, dining, and live entertainment aboard vessels designed to function as self-contained resorts rather than conventional transports.

The defining feature of Tsouderos is where it operates. Its ships deliberately spend most of their time **outside normal planetary and system jurisdictions**, traveling through free space where local gambling restrictions generally do not apply. This allows Tsouderos to offer games and services that might be prohibited or heavily regulated at many of the worlds its passengers come from. The arrangement is legal within the corridors in which the fleet operates, and Tsouderos maintains the necessary licenses for its free-navigation business.

Each vessel contains extensive **gaming decks**, including traditional table games, live dealers, private rooms, and high-stakes facilities. Gambling is the central attraction, but the ships are also full-service luxury destinations with restaurants, bars, performance venues, lounges, and private accommodations.

Tsouderos maintains extensive **VIP services**, including private suites, restricted gaming areas, personal service, and invitation-based events. Its loyalty program provides frequent and high-value customers with additional privileges, while the company's security division places unusual emphasis on discretion and guest privacy.

The fleet operates continuously rather than traveling to a permanent resort destination. Passengers board and disembark at scheduled points along established routes, while the casino itself continues moving from corridor to corridor. This practice is the origin of the company's well-known description of itself as **"The House Never Docks."**

Tsouderos currently advertises **six luxury casino ships**, more than **40 free-space routes**, over **350 gaming tables**, and a combined fleet capacity of approximately **12,000 guests**.
