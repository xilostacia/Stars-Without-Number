**Sbokos** is a civilian snack, toy, and consumer-goods company best known for its distinctive smiling tofu mascot and bright Japanese-inspired branding. Its products are aimed heavily at families and younger consumers, and the Sbokos character has become recognizable far beyond the company's original markets.

The company's food division produces packaged snacks in a wide range of flavors and formats. Although the mascot is based on tofu, Sbokos is not exclusively a tofu-food company. The character functions primarily as the corporate mascot across a broader catalog of snack foods.

Sbokos also operates a substantial **toy and merchandise business**. Plush toys, figures, keychains, children's products, collectibles, and licensed merchandise featuring the mascot are sold alongside the company's food products. Many product lines deliberately cross between the two divisions, with limited snacks accompanied by collectible packaging, toys, or promotional items.

Its **Collections** division handles merchandise intended more for collectors than children, including limited editions, themed product runs, accessories, and branded household goods. The company regularly uses seasonal and regional releases to keep its mascot and product lines commercially visible.

Sbokos maintains a deliberately light and cheerful public image centered on small everyday comforts rather than prestige or technical sophistication. Its stores and products are common civilian sights in populated colonies, stations, and commercial districts, making Sbokos one of the more immediately recognizable consumer brands in settled space.
