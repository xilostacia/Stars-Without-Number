The **K'kree** are large herd animals found on a number of inhabited worlds. They are closely related to the [[G'naak]], with both species descending from the same broad ancestral stock. Where the G'naak developed into active predators, the K'kree evolved along a herbivorous prey-animal branch. Their shared ancestry remains obvious in their general body structure, but K'kree are heavier, broader, and built for endurance, herd movement, and defense rather than pursuit.

## Biology
K'kree are large quadrupeds with a raised forebody and a pair of smaller forelimbs capable of limited manipulation. Their sensory organs are arranged primarily to provide broad awareness of their surroundings rather than the concentrated forward vision of the G'naak. This, combined with their strong hearing and herd behavior, makes them difficult to approach unnoticed. They are powerful animals despite being prey species. Adult K'kree can be dangerous when frightened, cornered, protecting young, or caught in a herd panic. Their coloration and size vary considerably between populations.

## Ecology
K'kree are almost entirely herbivorous. Wild populations usually live in large herds and spend much of their time grazing or moving between feeding grounds. Individuals rely heavily on the presence of others and become visibly distressed when separated from the herd for extended periods. Their ancestors shared many environments with the predatory ancestors of the [[G'naak]], producing two closely related animal groups occupying opposite sides of the same ecological relationship.

## Domestication
K'kree are among the more common large alien livestock species. Their herd instincts make them relatively easy to manage in groups, and domesticated populations are raised for meat, hides, milk-like secretions, transport, and other agricultural purposes depending upon local culture and breed. Some frontier settlements maintain enormous free-ranging herds rather than enclosed farms, using vehicles, drones, or mounted handlers to move them between grazing areas. Domesticated K'kree can become accustomed to humans and other species, but their size and panic response still make careless handling dangerous.

## G'naak
K'kree show an unusually strong fear response toward [[G'naak]]. This behavior appears to be deeply rooted rather than learned entirely through experience. Even domesticated K'kree populations with no previous exposure to G'naak may react strongly to their scent, calls, or appearance. In environments where both species remain wild, G'naak frequently prey upon K'kree and similar herd animals.

## Human Use
K'kree are common enough that many human worlds simply regard them as another form of livestock. They may be found on ranches, agricultural colonies, frontier settlements, transport operations, zoological collections, or maintained as large grazing animals on suitable worlds. Individual breeds have been selected for different purposes, producing substantial variation in size, temperament, endurance, and production.
