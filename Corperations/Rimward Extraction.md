**Rimward Extraction** is an independent mining and resource company specializing in large-scale operations throughout the outer systems. Its business centers on locating, extracting, processing, and transporting the raw materials required by settlements, industry, infrastructure projects, and manufacturing networks across inhabited space.

The company is particularly associated with **asteroid mining**, operating extraction platforms and mobile industrial facilities in regions where conventional planetary mining is impractical or uneconomical. Rimward crews work alongside heavy automated machinery, processing equipment, and dedicated transport vessels designed for sustained operation far from major population centers.

Rather than shipping completely unprocessed ore whenever possible, Rimward performs much of its **initial refining and material separation on-site or in orbit**. This reduces unnecessary transport mass and allows processed industrial feedstock to enter wider supply networks more efficiently.

Its major commodities include **iron alloys, nickel, cobalt, rare elements, and volatile materials**, though individual operations vary considerably depending on the composition of the asteroid field or resource zone being exploited.

Rimward maintains a practical reputation built around difficult environments, experienced crews, and long-duration industrial operations. Its work is rarely visible to the average civilian, but much of the construction, manufacturing, energy production, and infrastructure of settled space ultimately depends upon the materials supplied by companies like Rimward.
