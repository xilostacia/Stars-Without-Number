**Orrego Transit** is a large civilian transportation company operating an integrated passenger network across **12 star systems**. Its services range from local point-to-point travel to scheduled planetary transit, interstellar passenger lines, and long-duration leisure voyages, allowing travelers to move through much of settled space under a single carrier.

At the local level, the company operates **Orrego Taxi**, an on-demand service connecting stations, moons, nearby colonies, and other short-range destinations. These craft fill much the same role as conventional taxis and regional shuttles, particularly in areas where settlements are too dispersed for permanent transit lines.

Within heavily populated systems, Orrego maintains scheduled **System Transit** networks linking major cities, stations, ports, habitats, and orbital facilities. These services are designed around frequent departures and standardized connections, forming part of the everyday transportation infrastructure in regions where private spacecraft ownership is impractical.

For longer journeys, Orrego operates dedicated **Interstellar Lines** with larger passenger vessels designed for comfort during extended travel between systems. The company also runs **Cruise Voyages**, multi-stop passenger routes built around tourism and leisure rather than simple transportation.

Orrego advertises more than **4,800 destinations** across its network, carries millions of passengers annually, and reports an average **99.6% on-time arrival rate**. Its size has made its schedules and terminals a routine part of civilian life across many of the systems in which it operates.
