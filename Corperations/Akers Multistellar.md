**Akers Multistellar** is a large infrastructure and habitation company specializing in the construction of permanent communities in remote, orbital, and newly developed regions. Its work combines modular habitats, industrial infrastructure, automation, and long-term support services into systems intended to remain useful as settlements grow.

The company is best known for modular habitation complexes. Akers habitats are designed to begin as relatively small installations and expand over time as population and industrial demand increase. Residential areas, workplaces, life-support systems, utilities, storage, and public spaces can be added without requiring the original settlement to be dismantled or extensively rebuilt.

Akers also constructs the supporting infrastructure around those habitats. Power distribution, utilities, cargo handling, maintenance facilities, industrial spaces, and orbital structures are commonly included in larger contracts. The company frequently provides complete settlement packages rather than individual buildings, particularly for clients establishing new operations in locations where local construction capacity is limited.

Automation is central to Akers' approach. Robotic construction equipment, cargo-handling systems, remote maintenance platforms, and industrial machinery allow relatively small teams to assemble and operate installations that would otherwise require much larger workforces. Akers manufactures many of these machines itself and sells some systems separately to industrial and government customers.

Unlike companies specializing primarily in temporary expeditionary bases, Akers markets its projects as places where people are expected to remain. Civilian housing and long-term quality of life are given significant attention alongside industrial requirements. This has made the company particularly common in developing colonies, remote industrial settlements, research communities, and orbital habitats transitioning from temporary installations into permanent population centers.

Akers provides long-term maintenance and expansion services after construction is complete. Many settlements continue purchasing replacement modules, additional infrastructure, and technical support from the company for decades after their initial deployment.

Its operations overlap with companies such as [[Tavares Industrial]] in modular infrastructure, [[Messia]] in enclosed living environments, and larger settlement-development firms such as [[Couloumbis Alliance]] and [[Wayfarer Megacorp]]. Akers is distinguished primarily by its focus on modular habitation, automation, and the gradual expansion of permanent communities.
