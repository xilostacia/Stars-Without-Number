**STELLA** is a major live-entertainment company specializing in concerts, touring productions, performance venues, talent management, and large-scale immersive entertainment. Its productions operate across numerous settled worlds, making the company one of the more visible civilian entertainment brands in interstellar space.

The company organizes **galaxy-spanning tours**, coordinating performers, equipment, production crews, transportation, and venue schedules across multiple systems. These productions range from conventional concerts and stage performances to enormous events designed specifically for orbital arenas and other high-capacity venues.

STELLA also maintains its own **venue network**, with performance spaces located on many of the most heavily traveled and economically important worlds. These facilities range from conventional theaters and clubs to vast arenas capable of accommodating audiences measured in the tens of thousands.

Its **Talent Services** division works with artists, performers, producers, and other entertainment professionals. STELLA can provide management, production support, touring logistics, promotion, and access to its venue network, allowing successful performers to reach audiences far beyond their home system.

The company is particularly associated with **immersive productions** using holography, spatial projection, synchronized environments, and other technologies that allow performances to extend beyond a conventional physical stage. Major STELLA events are frequently designed as complete sensory experiences rather than simple concerts.

STELLA reports operating venues on **42 worlds** and running productions across **six sectors**. Its scale allows the company to move major acts between distant population centers while maintaining consistent technical and production standards.

Among its advertised productions is **Echoes in Orbit**, a holographic concert experience starring **Kael Rien**, demonstrating the type of large-scale technologically intensive performance for which the company has become known.
