**Compass Group** is an exploration, survey, and planetary-intelligence company specializing in the assessment of worlds and systems before large-scale settlement or development begins. Active since **2187**, the company has built its reputation on providing governments, colonial organizations, and industrial clients with the information needed to decide whether a location is worth the cost and risk of expansion.

Compass conducts orbital surveys, planetary mapping, geological assessment, atmospheric studies, biosphere analysis, resource identification, and environmental hazard evaluation. Its survey fleets combine crewed exploration vessels with autonomous probes capable of remaining in-system for extended periods while collecting data.

Rather than simply producing maps, Compass develops complete environmental and operational profiles. A typical assessment may include surface conditions, climate behavior, available resources, natural hazards, potential settlement zones, access routes, and the infrastructure likely to be required for permanent habitation. These reports are frequently used during the earliest stages of colonial planning.

The company also maintains extensive probe networks and remote-monitoring systems. These allow Compass to continue observing newly surveyed locations after the initial expedition has departed, identifying seasonal changes, geological instability, atmospheric shifts, or other conditions that could alter the suitability of a prospective colony.

Compass data is widely used by settlement developers, infrastructure companies, industrial concerns, and governments planning expansion into less-developed regions. Companies such as [[Couloumbis Alliance]], [[Akers Multistellar]], and [[Wayfarer Megacorp]] operate in markets where detailed pre-settlement surveys are particularly valuable, though Compass itself generally remains focused on exploration and intelligence rather than construction.

Its public reputation is based heavily on neutrality and accuracy. Because extremely expensive development decisions can depend on its findings, Compass emphasizes independent assessment rather than telling clients what they want to hear. A negative Compass report has ended more than one proposed colonial or industrial project before construction began.
