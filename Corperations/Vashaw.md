**Vashaw** is one of the oldest surviving industrial manufacturers in human space, tracing its origins to **2063** and retaining a distinctly Russian corporate identity more than a millennium later. Its modern operations center on large-scale manufacturing for settlements, stations, industrial facilities, transport networks, and spacecraft.

Rather than specializing in a single market, Vashaw produces the heavy industrial foundation used by numerous other organizations. Its major divisions include **propulsion systems, shipbuilding, station systems, industrial equipment, transport and logistics, and structural materials**. Vashaw equipment is generally associated with standardized construction, durability, large production runs, and designs intended to remain serviceable for long periods.

The company operates more than **200 manufacturing facilities across 12 star systems** and employs roughly **550,000 people**. Its facilities range from conventional factories to major orbital construction complexes capable of producing spacecraft, station sections, engines, industrial machinery, freight equipment, and structural components at enormous scale.

Vashaw's age has made its equipment unusually widespread. Entire stations and industrial settlements may contain systems descended from Vashaw designs generations apart, while newer products continue to use standardized components and engineering principles developed across centuries of production. The company's reputation rests less on novelty than on manufacturing capacity, compatibility, and equipment built for demanding industrial use.
