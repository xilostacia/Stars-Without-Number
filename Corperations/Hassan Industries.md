**Hassan Industries** is a major manufacturer of standardized spacecraft, station, vehicle, and industrial components. Rather than building most complete vessels itself, Hassan supplies many of the parts and subsystems used by manufacturers, repair yards, infrastructure operators, and independent engineering firms across settled space.

Its product catalog covers nearly every major mechanical and electrical system required by modern spacecraft and orbital infrastructure. Common Hassan components include propulsion assemblies, power systems, structural sections, interface hardware, life-support units, navigation equipment, thermal-management systems, actuators, electrical assemblies, and auxiliary machinery.

Standardization is central to the company's business model. Hassan produces large quantities of compatible components designed to fit widely used industrial specifications, making its parts common replacements for damaged or outdated equipment. For less conventional designs, the company also offers custom and specialty systems manufactured to client specifications.

This combination of standardized and custom production has made Hassan particularly important to repair yards and long-lived infrastructure. A ship or station does not need to have been built by Hassan for substantial portions of its machinery to eventually contain Hassan replacements.

The company supplies civilian, commercial, industrial, exploration, and military customers. Freight operators, station authorities, shipyards, mining concerns, colonial infrastructure projects, and defense contractors all make use of Hassan components, though the company itself is primarily identified as an industrial supplier rather than a military manufacturer.

Hassan parts have a reputation for compatibility and availability rather than exceptional performance. Engineers often choose them because replacement units, documentation, and equivalent components can be sourced across a broad range of markets.

Its products are frequently found alongside equipment produced by companies such as [[Terra Prime Unity]], [[Vashaw]], [[Ad Astra Enterprises]], and [[Silverlight]]. Hassan's position in the market is less about competing with those firms directly and more about supplying the components that keep their ships, stations, and industrial systems operational.
