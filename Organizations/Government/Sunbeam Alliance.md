**Sunbeam Alliance** is a system-wide governing alliance linking multiple inhabited worlds, stations, and major transit routes under a shared civil administration. Rather than replacing its member governments outright, the Alliance coordinates matters that require cooperation across the entire system, including trade regulation, customs, transportation standards, infrastructure, civil records, and mutual security.

Its member worlds retain their own communities and local governments while participating in common institutions intended to keep movement and commerce between them predictable. Alliance authorities maintain shared transit rules, customs procedures, commercial standards, and public-service systems used by travelers and businesses operating between member territories.

The Alliance also maintains a **civil registry** used for identification, residency records, and other administrative services throughout the system. Infrastructure standards, communications networks, orbital facilities, and major transportation links are coordinated at the system level where necessary.

Security is organized around cooperation between member governments rather than a single centralized military authority. Alliance institutions coordinate emergency response, infrastructure protection, and mutual security operations, while local governments and their own forces continue to operate within their jurisdictions.

The principal member worlds shown in public Alliance material include [[Aurelia]], the system's capital world; industrial [[Vesper]]; research-centered [[Thalox]]; agricultural [[Restari]]; mining world [[Myr]]; and [[Halkyren]], known for major habitat infrastructure. [[Rhea Station]] functions as an important trade hub within the same network.

Sunbeam presents itself primarily as the administrative framework holding an already interconnected star system together. For most residents, its presence is encountered through travel regulations, customs, identification systems, trade rules, public infrastructure, and the standards that allow movement between its different worlds.
