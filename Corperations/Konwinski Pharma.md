**Konwinski Pharma** is an interstellar pharmaceutical manufacturer and medical-supply company founded in **2291** and headquartered at [[Virel Station]] in the [[Kestral System]]. The company manufactures and distributes medicines to hospitals, clinics, humanitarian organizations, commercial operators, and remote settlements across established and frontier regions.

Its catalog includes generic medicines, specialized pharmaceuticals, emergency medical kits, controlled therapeutics, and thousands of other clinical products. Konwinski also performs contract manufacturing for outside organizations, allowing hospitals, governments, and other pharmaceutical firms to commission large production runs through its facilities.

Distribution is as important to the company as manufacturing. Konwinski operates specialized cold-chain and regulated-cargo networks designed to keep temperature-sensitive or tightly controlled drugs viable during long interstellar transit. This logistics capability allows it to service isolated stations and colonies that cannot maintain large local pharmaceutical industries.

The company supports more than **480 clinics and hospitals**, operates **12 manufacturing sites**, and distributes more than **6,300 product variants**, including over **40 classes of licensed controlled therapeutics**. Its ability to move essential medicines through difficult routes has made Konwinski an important supplier in regions where ordinary commercial distribution is unreliable.

Konwinski Pharma has also attracted persistent scrutiny because of alleged connections to the [[Konwinski]] Crime Family. Investigations have examined claims that legitimate pharmaceutical infrastructure, logistics networks, financial resources, or corporate entities have been exploited by criminal affiliates. Konwinski Pharma publicly denies involvement in illegal activity, states that its operations are independent of the family network, and maintains that it cooperates with relevant authorities. No direct criminal charges against the pharmaceutical company are publicly established in its corporate materials.

Despite the controversy, Konwinski remains a major medical supplier, particularly in remote regions where its logistics network can matter as much as the medicines themselves.
