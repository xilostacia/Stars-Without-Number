**Pippenger** is an industrial robotics and mechanized-systems manufacturer specializing in machines designed to extend human labor into environments where ordinary workers would be inefficient, endangered, or physically incapable of performing the required work.

Its best-known products are **exo frames**, wearable mechanical systems that increase a worker's strength, endurance, and precision while leaving the operator in direct control. These systems are common in construction, maintenance, cargo handling, industrial repair, and other forms of heavy manual work.

For jobs requiring greater lifting capacity, Pippenger produces larger **worker mechs**. These machines function as heavy industrial labor platforms capable of handling cargo, equipment, structural components, and other loads beyond the practical limits of conventional powered suits.

The company also manufactures **inspection drones** for automated or remotely operated monitoring, particularly in hazardous industrial facilities and difficult-to-reach areas. Smaller **support robotics** perform maintenance, inspection, logistics, and routine facility work, while **remote loaders** allow heavy materials and cargo to be moved without requiring personnel to remain near dangerous machinery or unstable environments.

Pippenger's machines are designed primarily as industrial tools rather than autonomous replacements for workers. The company places heavy emphasis on human-operated or human-supervised systems, modular construction, field repairability, and long service life.

More than **2,500 Pippenger systems** are reportedly in active service across **12 planetary regions**, with the company claiming an average operational uptime of **94.7%**. Its equipment is used by industrial, construction, logistics, infrastructure, and resource-extraction organizations throughout settled space.
