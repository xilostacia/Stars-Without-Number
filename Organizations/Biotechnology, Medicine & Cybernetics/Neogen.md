**Neogen** is an interstellar biotechnology and pharmaceutical company headquartered at [[Ceres Station]] in the [[Ceres Belt]]. Its work focuses on advanced biological medicine, particularly treatments intended to repair, replace, or deliberately modify biological function.

The company develops pharmaceuticals ranging from conventional targeted medicines to highly specialized biologics designed for genetically, environmentally, or physiologically distinct populations. Its clinical programs include treatments tailored for people living under unusual colonial conditions, where differences in gravity, atmosphere, radiation exposure, diet, or long-term isolation can produce medical problems rarely encountered on Earth.

Gene therapy is one of Neogen's major specialties. Its treatments intervene directly at the genetic and cellular level, correcting inherited disorders, altering cellular behavior, and adapting therapies to individual patients. Neogen is also heavily involved in regenerative medicine, including accelerated tissue repair, cellular replacement, organ reconstruction, and treatments intended to restore biological functions that would otherwise be permanently lost.

Its biofabrication division manufactures living tissue for transplantation, medicine, research, and industrial applications. Neogen laboratories can produce engineered biological materials ranging from replacement tissue to complex organ structures, allowing some injuries and diseases to be treated by physically rebuilding damaged biological systems rather than simply managing them.

Neogen also operates large-scale clinical manufacturing and distribution programs, allowing complex biological therapies to be supplied far beyond major medical centers. This places it in the same broad medical sector as [[Konwinski Pharma]], while its regenerative and biological-modification work overlaps with organizations such as [[Barsalou Circle]] and [[Guo Yin Company]].

### The Githiaskio Uplift
Neogen's most famous achievement is also one of the most consequential biological experiments in human history: the creation of the [[Githiaskio]], humanity's first stable uplifted sophont species.

The project began roughly two centuries ago using terrestrial octopuses as its biological foundation. Rather than attempting to reshape them into humanoids, Neogen concentrated on the limitations that had prevented naturally occurring octopuses from developing a technological civilization. Researchers extended lifespan, eliminated the reproductive processes responsible for post-breeding death, altered developmental and social behavior, expanded neurological capacity, and developed reliable methods of abstract communication between generations.

The resulting Githiaskio remained unmistakably cephalopods. They retained eight limbs, distributed neural structures, sophisticated camouflage and color-changing abilities, and an aquatic physiology, but possessed stable human-comparable sapience and vastly increased lifespans.

The first viable populations were originally raised inside Neogen research environments. Over subsequent generations the Githiaskio developed independent communities, reproduced without corporate intervention, and became legally recognized as a distinct sapient people. Neogen no longer produces Githiaskio, though the company's role in their creation remains one of the defining events in its history.

The early uplift program remains controversial. Historical disputes concerning experimental subjects, legal personhood, genetic ownership, corporate patents, and Neogen's authority over the earliest generations continue to influence how the company is viewed by Githiaskio communities.

### The Primate Barrier
The success of the [[Githiaskio]] project was particularly unexpected because Neogen had previously attempted uplift programs involving several species believed to be much closer to human intelligence.

Chimpanzees, bonobos, gorillas, orangutans, and other primates were extensively studied and genetically modified. Researchers successfully increased memory, language comprehension, planning ability, lifespan, coordination, and problem-solving capacity. Some experimental populations became extraordinarily intelligent by animal standards.

None became stable sophonts.

Attempts to push modified primates beyond a persistent cognitive threshold repeatedly resulted in developmental failure, neurological instability, severe behavioral problems, or simply no further increase in abstract intelligence.

The phenomenon became informally known as the **Primate Barrier**.

Despite centuries of advances in genetics and neuroscience, there is still no generally accepted explanation for why humanity was able to create a sapient cephalopod while repeated attempts to achieve the apparently easier task of uplifting closely related primates failed.

Neogen has also conducted research involving other highly intelligent terrestrial animals, but the Githiaskio remain the only publicly confirmed successful Neogen uplift.
