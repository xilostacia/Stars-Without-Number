The **Githiaskio** are a sapient uplift species descended from terrestrial octopuses and created through the biotechnology programs of [[Neogen]] roughly two centuries ago. They are not extraterrestrial life, though they are often casually grouped with alien species due to their appearance, physiology, and relative rarity. They are humanity's first and, so far, only widely recognized successful uplift. Unlike many speculative uplift projects, the Githiaskio were not redesigned to resemble humans. They remain unmistakably cephalopods, with eight flexible limbs, soft bodies, distributed nervous systems, exceptional camouflage, and an entirely aquatic physiology.

## Origin
The first successful Githiaskio populations emerged from a long-running [[Neogen]] research program intended to determine whether nonhuman terrestrial life could be deliberately elevated to stable sapience.

Octopuses were not originally considered the easiest candidate. Earlier programs had concentrated heavily on primates and other animals with seemingly obvious cognitive similarities to humans. Those efforts repeatedly failed to produce stable sophonts, a phenomenon eventually known as the [[Primate Barrier]]. Cephalopods proved unexpectedly different.

Neogen researchers found that octopuses already possessed many of the traits they were attempting to create: complex problem solving, tool use, environmental manipulation, observational learning, curiosity, sophisticated sensory processing, and an unusually decentralized nervous system. The project instead concentrated on the biological limitations that prevented those traits from accumulating into civilization.

These included extremely short lifespans, reproductive death, limited long-term social structures, inconsistent intergenerational learning, and neurological development poorly suited to sustained abstract communication. Over many generations, Neogen modified these systems rather than replacing them. The first stable Githiaskio population appeared approximately two centuries ago.

## Biology
Adult Githiaskio vary considerably in size, but most possess a body mass somewhere within the broad range of a large terrestrial octopus. They possess eight highly dexterous limbs lined with manipulatory suckers. The limbs are capable of both powerful gross movement and extremely precise manipulation, allowing a Githiaskio to operate tools that would require several human hands simultaneously.

Their nervous systems remain heavily distributed. A large amount of sensory processing and motor coordination takes place throughout the limbs rather than exclusively within the central brain. This gives them an unusual relationship with their own bodies. Individual limbs can perform complicated tasks with only limited conscious attention, while the central mind concentrates elsewhere. 

Their skin retains sophisticated chromatophores capable of rapidly changing color, pattern, and contrast. Many Githiaskio can also alter skin texture to a lesser degree. Camouflage remains possible, but in modern Githiaskio society these abilities are at least as important for communication as concealment.

Neogen dramatically extended their natural lifespan. Modern Githiaskio commonly live for many decades, though their exact life expectancy depends heavily on environment, genetics, and medical access. The reproductive cycle that causes natural octopuses to deteriorate and die after breeding was also removed. Githiaskio can reproduce multiple times and remain healthy afterward.

## Communication
Githiaskio communication is inherently multimodal.
Their natural language combines:
- skin coloration
- shifting patterns
- body posture
- limb position
- tactile gestures
- movement
- sound produced through specialized artificial or biological mechanisms

A conversation between two Githiaskio can contain information transmitted simultaneously through several different channels. Color is particularly important. Rapid changes in hue, contrast, and pattern can communicate emotional state, emphasis, grammatical structure, or meaning. Humans can learn simplified Githiaskio languages, but without specialized equipment they physically cannot reproduce much of the visual component. Likewise, a Githiaskio communicating with humans usually relies on vocalizers, displays, translation software, or standardized gestural systems. Human languages are entirely learnable to them. Speaking them naturally is the problem. Many Githiaskio therefore carry small electronic vocalization devices when living among humans.

## Intelligence
Githiaskio cognition is recognizably sapient but does not simply reproduce human thought in a different body. Their distributed neural architecture allows them to divide attention unusually well. A Githiaskio may manipulate several objects, monitor its surroundings, maintain a visual conversation, and perform another task simultaneously without experiencing the same degree of cognitive interference a human would.

This has sometimes produced the exaggerated stereotype that Githiaskio can "think eight thoughts at once." They cannot. Their limbs are not independent people. However, they are exceptionally good at coordinating parallel physical tasks. Their spatial reasoning is also generally excellent, particularly in three-dimensional environments. This is partly biological and partly cultural, as Githiaskio normally live and move through water rather than across flat surfaces.

## Aquatic Life
Githiaskio breathe water. This remains the single largest obstacle to integration into predominantly human environments. Githiaskio communities usually inhabit flooded habitats, oceanic settlements, aquatic sections of mixed-species stations, or specially constructed water environments. A human-style room converted for Githiaskio habitation is typically filled completely with temperature-controlled oxygenated water. Mixed settlements frequently contain both dry and flooded sections connected through pressure and transition chambers.

### Environmental Suits
Githiaskio can operate in dry environments using **aquatic mobility suits**. These suits maintain a circulating water environment around the animal while allowing movement through air-filled spaces. Simple models resemble mobile transparent tanks or flexible pressure envelopes.

More advanced models incorporate:
- powered locomotion
- manipulator extensions
- communication systems
- environmental monitoring
- armor
- tool mounts
- medical support

A Githiaskio aboard a human ship can therefore function normally, but accommodating one requires more preparation than simply assigning another bunk. Emergency failure of an aquatic suit can become life-threatening very quickly.

## Society
The earliest Githiaskio knew no civilization except humanity's. Their first languages, tools, educational systems, legal concepts, and scientific knowledge were all developed within human-controlled research environments. That changed rapidly. Within several generations, Githiaskio communities began creating institutions adapted specifically to their own biology and cognition.

Modern Githiaskio culture is therefore neither an imitation of human civilization nor some rediscovered "natural octopus culture." It is something historically new. Their societies are only around two centuries old. Some traditions practiced today can be traced to identifiable individuals who participated in the first stable generations.

That makes Githiaskio historical consciousness very different from that of humanity. Their oldest recorded political arguments, artistic movements, religious debates, and philosophical schools are all relatively recent. Many Githiaskio have living family traditions reaching directly back to the earliest Neogen populations.

## Communities
Githiaskio remain uncommon compared with humans. Most live in relatively small aquatic communities located within larger human-controlled systems, though several independent Githiaskio settlements now exist. Oceanic planets and moons are especially attractive to them.

Human colonies often value Githiaskio residents in industries where aquatic or zero-gravity environments are common, though modern Githiaskio generally dislike being reduced to occupational stereotypes.

Their abilities make them particularly effective in:
- underwater construction
- ship maintenance
- delicate mechanical work
- salvag
- laboratory research
- medicine
- zero-gravity operations
- complex manual assembly

They are also extremely capable pilots once controls have been adapted to their physiology.

## Technology
Githiaskio technological development began with human technology, but their engineering has increasingly diverged. Human machinery assumes hands, rigid skeletons, dry environments, and primarily two-dimensional movement. Githiaskio machinery does not. Their control systems frequently use large surfaces containing many simultaneous touch points rather than discrete buttons or keyboards. Tools may be designed to be held by several limbs at once. Visual interfaces often use color and pattern much more aggressively than human designs.

Their underwater environments make conventional electronics and machinery unnecessarily inconvenient, encouraging extensive development of sealed systems, soft robotics, pressure-resistant equipment, and fluid-compatible interfaces.

Their spacecraft are particularly distinctive. A purpose-built Githiaskio vessel may contain almost no air-filled interior at all. To humans, boarding one without environmental equipment feels more like entering a submarine that has intentionally flooded itself.

## Relationship with Neogen
The relationship between the Githiaskio and [[Neogen]] is complicated. Neogen created them. That fact cannot be separated from their history. For some Githiaskio, the company is remembered as the institution responsible for giving their species existence and the ability to build a civilization. For others, Neogen is remembered as a corporation that experimented on their ancestors before those ancestors possessed recognized legal personhood. Both interpretations can be true.

Historical records from the earliest uplift program include disputes over laboratory ownership, genetic patents, experimental control, reproductive rights, and the legal status of the first demonstrably sapient individuals. Those disputes eventually contributed to some of the foundational laws governing artificial sapience, uplifted organisms, and genetically created people throughout human space. Modern Githiaskio are not owned, manufactured, or controlled by Neogen. They reproduce independently and maintain their own institutions.

## Personhood
The Githiaskio forced humanity to answer a question that had previously been largely theoretical:
**Can humanity create another people?**

Initial legal recognition varied dramatically between governments. Some recognized Githiaskio personhood almost immediately after sapience was conclusively demonstrated. Others initially classified them as genetically engineered animals, research organisms, or corporate biological property. Those classifications did not survive.

Today, virtually every major human government recognizes the Githiaskio as sapient persons, though the exact legal category used varies between jurisdictions. The political battles surrounding their recognition remain foundational events in modern bioethics.

## Identity
Many Githiaskio dislike being called aliens. Others do not care. Biologically, they are terrestrial life. Historically, they are products of human civilization. Culturally, they are increasingly something independent of either category. Among scientists, the normal classification is **uplifted sophont**. Among ordinary spacers, they are frequently just called **Giths**. Whether Githiaskio themselves appreciate that abbreviation depends heavily on the individual.

## Relations with Humans
Githiaskio generally understand humans far better than humans understand most extraterrestrial species. Their civilization developed within humanity's. They grow up surrounded by human languages, history, entertainment, technology, politics, and institutions. Human behavior is therefore not especially mysterious to them. The reverse is less consistently true.

A Githiaskio can display complicated emotional information across its skin without a nearby human noticing anything beyond a color change. Humans sometimes misinterpret stillness as boredom, rapid limb movement as agitation, camouflage responses as fear, or color displays as decorative. Mixed crews usually learn each other's habits quickly.

## Relations with Extraterrestrials
The existence of the Githiaskio produces a peculiar reaction among genuinely extraterrestrial civilizations. To humanity, they are an extraordinary biotechnology achievement. To aliens, the more remarkable fact is often that humans intentionally created another sapient species only a few centuries after developing interstellar civilization. The [[Hivers]] have shown particular interest in Githiaskio development, both biologically and socially. Whether this attention is flattering or concerning depends on who is asked.

## Playability
Githiaskio are fully suitable as player characters, though they require more environmental support than most species. They breathe water rather than air, so a mixed-species crew must provide either flooded living quarters or a functioning aquatic mobility suit for extended time aboard human-designed ships and stations.

Their eight limbs, distributed nervous system, excellent spatial awareness, camouflage abilities, and capacity for handling several physical tasks at once make them particularly effective technicians, pilots, scientists, salvagers, medics, engineers, and zero-gravity operators. Their physiology should provide meaningful advantages without treating each limb as a completely independent action.

Githiaskio characters are culturally diverse. They are a young species whose civilization developed within human space, so they can plausibly come from independent Githiaskio communities, mixed human settlements, corporate habitats, research institutions, ships, or frontier colonies.

Their most significant practical limitation is environmental dependence. Damage to an aquatic suit or loss of access to breathable water can become a serious emergency, making life-support equipment considerably more important to a Githiaskio character than it is to most humans.
