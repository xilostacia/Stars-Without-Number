**Panstellar Multistellar** is a large agricultural and logistics company built around one idea: controlling as much of the food supply chain as possible from initial cultivation to final distribution. Rather than specializing in farming alone, Panstellar operates an integrated network covering agricultural production, food processing, freight, storage, and market supply across multiple star systems.

Its agricultural operations include conventional planetary farming alongside controlled-environment facilities on moons, stations, and orbital habitats. These facilities are designed for dependable high-yield production in places where open-air agriculture is impossible or unreliable.

Panstellar also operates large-scale food-processing infrastructure, converting harvested crops into products suitable for long-distance transport and extended storage. Much of this output is intended for settlements, stations, and other populations that cannot reliably produce enough food locally.

The company's freight division moves agricultural products and processed food between systems, with specialized vessels and cargo infrastructure for temperature-sensitive or otherwise perishable goods. This transport network connects directly with Panstellar's agricultural and processing facilities, reducing the number of independent contractors involved in moving its products.

Panstellar maintains extensive **strategic food reserves** throughout its operating territory. Large storage installations hold preserved food and agricultural products for later distribution, allowing supplies to be released during shortages, transportation disruptions, population growth, or other periods of increased demand.

Because Panstellar controls production, processing, shipping, and storage within the same corporate network, it has become an important supplier to colonies and governments concerned with long-duration food security. The company is headquartered at [[Ceres Station]] in the [[Sol System]] and operates across multiple settled systems.
