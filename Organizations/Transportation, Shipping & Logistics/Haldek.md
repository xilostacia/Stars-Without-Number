**Haldek** is a small **Kynari-owned armed transportation company** specializing in the secure movement of passengers and valuable cargo across dangerous or unreliable routes. Unlike large civilian carriers, Haldek operates almost entirely through private contracts, working directly with individual clients to plan each journey around the people, cargo, destination, and risks involved.

The company is extremely new and currently operates only **around a dozen ships**, all crewed entirely by **Kynari**. Its vessels are modified transports rather than dedicated warships, carrying defensive weapons, upgraded sensors, secure compartments, reinforced cargo storage, and accommodations for passengers who require more protection than conventional transit can provide.

Haldek handles both **protected cargo and passenger transport**. Contracts can include executives, specialists, private clients, sensitive equipment, research material, expensive components, sealed consignments, or other shipments where security and chain of custody matter more than moving large quantities cheaply. Crews remain closely involved throughout loading, transit, transfers, and final delivery rather than simply handing a shipment into a larger freight network.

The company's armed crews are intended to protect the vessel, its passengers, and its cargo rather than function as mercenaries. Haldek favors careful route planning, controlled transfers, discretion, and avoiding trouble where possible, but its crews and ships are equipped to defend themselves when interception or violence cannot be avoided.

Haldek's small fleet keeps it highly selective about its clientele. It cannot compete with companies such as [[Orrego Transit]] on volume or regular service, and it does not attempt to. Its reputation instead depends on direct relationships, confidentiality, and accepting specialized contracts where a client wants the transport company itself to be responsible for getting both **them and their property safely from departure to arrival**.
