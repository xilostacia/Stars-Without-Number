**Overwatch Pact** is a private policing and security-enforcement organization operating across multiple star systems, stations, and major trade corridors. Governments, corporations, station authorities, and other recognized clients contract the organization to provide law enforcement and civil-security services where local authorities require additional personnel or where private administration is preferred.

Its most visible service is uniformed patrol work. Overwatch personnel police stations, commercial districts, transport hubs, trade corridors, and other high-traffic locations under contracts negotiated with the authority responsible for the area. Depending on the jurisdiction, these agreements can grant Overwatch personnel powers normally associated with public law enforcement.

The Pact also provides contract law enforcement, station security, investigations, customs enforcement, detainee transport, riot control, and emergency-response services. Its operations frequently extend beyond simple guard work into active policing, including searches, arrests, evidence collection, crowd control, and enforcement of local trade and customs law.

Overwatch authority is not universal. Its legal powers vary by jurisdiction and depend on local charters, government contracts, corporate agreements, or emergency mandates. In some regions it operates as a conventional private security contractor, while elsewhere it may function as the primary police force for an entire station or commercial zone.

The organization maintains its own investigative and intelligence capabilities, supporting criminal investigations, customs operations, threat assessment, and coordination between deployments. This allows Overwatch to operate across jurisdictional boundaries more easily than many local police forces.

Overwatch is a controversial presence in some communities. Supporters point to its professional standards, rapid deployment, and ability to provide experienced personnel where local security is weak. Critics argue that widespread dependence on the Pact gives a private organization excessive influence over public order and allows corporate clients to purchase capabilities normally associated with governments. Overwatch publicly maintains that its personnel operate under applicable law and that its role is limited to enforcing the authority granted by its contracts and charters.
