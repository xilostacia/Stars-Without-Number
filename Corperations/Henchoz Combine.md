**Henchoz Combine** is an integrated arcology developer and operator built around the idea that housing, employment, education, healthcare, food supply, and other essential services can be managed as parts of a single urban system. Its arcologies are designed as self-contained communities rather than collections of separate residential and commercial developments.

A Henchoz complex typically combines housing, workplaces, schools, medical facilities, food services, utilities, public areas, and internal transportation under centralized planning. Residents may live, work, study, and receive most routine services without leaving the broader Combine-managed environment.

Housing is one of the company's largest operations, ranging from standard residential units to long-term family accommodation. These districts are built alongside the services needed to support permanent populations rather than treated as temporary worker housing.

Henchoz also maintains its own nutrition and food-service systems. Large communal kitchens, supply networks, cafeterias, and controlled food-distribution programs support the population of each arcology. The company's goal is dependable access rather than luxury dining, particularly in locations where outside supply can be inconsistent.

Healthcare and education are similarly integrated into the community structure. Clinics and hospitals provide routine and preventative care, while schools and vocational programs train residents for both local employment and wider professional opportunities.

The Combine's model gives it unusually broad influence over the communities it operates. A single organization may be responsible for a resident's landlord, employer, medical provider, school system, and essential services. Supporters point to the stability and efficiency of this arrangement, while critics are wary of populations becoming too dependent on a single corporate administration.

Henchoz occupies some of the same development space as [[Couloumbis Alliance]] and [[Wayfarer Megacorp]], while its enclosed community design overlaps with companies such as [[Messia]] and [[Akers Multistellar]]. Its defining feature is the degree to which civilian life itself is integrated into one managed system.
