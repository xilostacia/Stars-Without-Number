**Barsalou Circle** is an interstellar medical technology company specializing in cybernetics, prosthetics, neural interfaces, sensory augmentation, and long-term rehabilitation systems. Founded in **2287**, the company has operated for centuries and is one of the older surviving names in human cybernetic medicine.

The company is headquartered at [[Vellor Station]] in the [[Corinth Reach]] and maintains clinical and manufacturing relationships across settled space. Barsalou's products range from conventional replacement limbs to sophisticated neural interfaces and sensory systems intended to restore or expand lost human function.

Its prosthetics division produces artificial limbs and support systems for both civilian and professional use. Barsalou emphasizes durability, serviceability, and natural control rather than highly specialized military enhancement. Products are commonly fitted through hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and licensed clinics rather than sold directly as consumer hardware.

Neural-interface technology forms another major part of the company's business. These systems connect prosthetics, sensory devices, mobility aids, and other medical hardware directly to the nervous system, allowing patients to operate replacements with increasingly natural levels of control. Related sensory systems include artificial or augmented visual, auditory, and tactile interfaces.

Barsalou also maintains extensive rehabilitation and clinical services. Patients receiving major cybernetic replacements may remain under company-supported care for months or years while hardware, software, and neural calibration are adjusted to the individual. This long-term treatment model has become one of the company's defining practices.

The company is generally associated with restorative medicine rather than elective augmentation. Its public messaging places strong emphasis on patient independence, medical oversight, and rebuilding lost capability. That distinction has helped Barsalou maintain favorable relationships with hospitals, governments, insurers, and regulators wary of uncontrolled human enhancement technologies.

Its work overlaps with [[Guo Yin Company]] in specialized long-term care and with medical and biological firms such as [[Neogen]] and [[Konwinski Pharma]], though Barsalou's primary expertise remains mechanical and neural cybernetics rather than pharmaceuticals or biotechnology.
