**Standridge Band** is a large agricultural and ranching company specializing in conventional planetary food production. Its operations combine crop farming, livestock, animal feed, food processing, and distribution, making the company a major supplier to settlements that possess enough usable land for large-scale agriculture.

The company cultivates **grains, oilseeds, specialty crops, and other staple agricultural products** for direct consumption, livestock feed, and industrial food production. Standridge farms often use extensive automation, including autonomous field equipment, monitoring systems, drones, and modern irrigation technology, but remain fundamentally based around conventional open-land agriculture rather than orbital or enclosed growing systems.

Standridge is also heavily involved in **livestock production**, maintaining breeding and ranching operations that supply meat, dairy, animal products, and breeding stock. Feed production is consequently a substantial part of its business, with dedicated grain storage and processing infrastructure supporting both its own herds and outside agricultural customers.

Its processing and distribution division handles crops and livestock products after harvest, operating storage facilities, packing plants, food-processing centers, and regional logistics networks. This allows Standridge to move products from rural production areas into cities, settlements, and wider commercial supply chains without relying entirely on outside processors.

The company places considerable emphasis on **land management and long-term agricultural productivity**. Soil quality, water availability, grazing conditions, habitat management, and continued fertility are treated as business assets that must remain productive over generations. This gives Standridge a reputation as a comparatively traditional agricultural company despite the advanced technology used across its farms.

Standridge facilities are most commonly found on established agricultural worlds and developing colonies with substantial usable land. Its combination of crops, livestock, feed, and processing makes it particularly valuable in regions attempting to reduce dependence on imported food.
