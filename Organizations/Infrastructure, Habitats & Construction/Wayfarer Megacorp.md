**Wayfarer Megacorp** is a massive diversified corporation involved in virtually every stage of human expansion into undeveloped space. Rather than concentrating on a single industry, Wayfarer combines **colony development, heavy industry, infrastructure, logistics, habitat construction, resource operations, and security** into large-scale settlement programs.

Wayfarer frequently works from the earliest stages of colonial development. Its operations can begin with surveying and resource exploitation, continue through industrial construction and orbital logistics, and eventually produce permanent settlements supported by transportation, utilities, habitat systems, security, and commercial infrastructure. This allows Wayfarer to function as both a contractor and a long-term economic presence on developing worlds.

Its major divisions include **Colony Development**, **Industrial Systems**, **Orbital Logistics**, **Habitat Infrastructure**, **Frontier Operations**, and **Security & Support**. These divisions allow the corporation to supply equipment and personnel internally instead of depending entirely on outside contractors, giving Wayfarer considerable control over projects it manages.

Wayfarer's published scale includes more than **420 colony contracts**, **27 major shipyards and industrial complexes**, over **190 logistics hubs**, operations in more than **60 systems**, and approximately **250,000 employees across eight sectors**.

Because of its size, Wayfarer facilities can resemble entire company-built settlements rather than ordinary corporate sites. On some frontier projects, Wayfarer may be responsible for the port, industrial district, housing infrastructure, freight network, resource operations, and security apparatus simultaneously. The corporation's business model is built around establishing infrastructure that remains profitable as a settlement develops from an isolated frontier operation into a permanent population center.
