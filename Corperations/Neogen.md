**Neogen** is an interstellar biotechnology and pharmaceutical company headquartered at [[Ceres Station]] in the [[Ceres Belt]]. Its work focuses on advanced biological medicine, particularly treatments intended to repair, replace, or deliberately modify human biological function.

The company develops pharmaceuticals ranging from conventional targeted medicines to highly specialized biologics designed for genetically, environmentally, or physiologically distinct populations. Its pharmaceutical research supports both general medicine and treatments tailored for people living under unusual colonial conditions.

Gene therapy is one of Neogen's major specialties. These treatments are designed to intervene directly at the genetic level, correcting inherited disorders, altering cellular behavior, or adapting therapies to individual patients. The company also develops cellular medicines intended to stimulate or regulate biological processes that conventional drugs cannot easily address.

Neogen's regenerative-medicine division concentrates on restoring damaged tissue and biological function. Its work includes treatments intended to accelerate natural repair, replace damaged cells, and support recovery from injuries or illnesses that would otherwise cause permanent impairment.

Biofabrication extends this work into the manufacture of living tissue. Neogen laboratories produce engineered biological materials for medical treatment, transplantation, research, and other applications. These technologies allow certain damaged tissues to be replaced or reconstructed rather than merely treated.

The company also operates large-scale clinical manufacturing and supply programs, allowing complex biological therapies to be produced and distributed beyond major medical centers. This places Neogen in the same broad medical sector as [[Konwinski Pharma]], while its regenerative and human-modification work also intersects with organizations such as [[Barsalou Circle]] and [[Guo Yin Company]].
