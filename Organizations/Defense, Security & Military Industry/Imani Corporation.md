**Imani Corporation** is a major weapons and tactical-equipment manufacturer serving civilian, private security, law-enforcement, and military customers. Its catalog is unusually broad, ranging from personal firearms and protective equipment to crew-served weapons, autonomous combat platforms, surveillance systems, and specialized tactical hardware.

Imani's civilian lines include rifles, sidearms, optics, protective gear, and other equipment intended for private security, professional, recreational, and home-defense markets. Its commercial reputation is built around durable, modular equipment rather than highly specialized boutique weapons.

The company's security division supplies corporate security forces, police organizations, private contractors, and similar clients. These systems include duty weapons, armor, shields, less-lethal equipment, surveillance hardware, breaching tools, and other equipment intended for sustained professional use.

Military and government procurement represents another major part of Imani's business. Its defense catalog includes heavier weapons, support weapons, remote platforms, unmanned systems, targeting equipment, and integrated tactical packages. Imani frequently sells complete equipment families rather than individual weapons, allowing clients to standardize firearms, optics, protective systems, and support hardware around common supply chains.

Remote systems have become an increasingly visible part of the company's catalog. Imani manufactures armed and unarmed UAVs, UGVs, automated observation platforms, and other systems intended to extend the reach of security and military personnel without requiring a human operator at every position.

The company also maintains a significant line of nonlethal systems for riot control, detention, facility security, and escalation management. These products allow Imani to supply organizations whose requirements range from ordinary civilian protection to full military operations.

Imani competes across several markets occupied by companies such as [[Hsiang Enterprises]], [[Hsiang Systems]], [[Magnus]], and [[Threat Circle]]. Unlike more specialized manufacturers, Imani's defining characteristic is the sheer breadth of its catalog and its ability to supply equipment for nearly every level of armed client.
