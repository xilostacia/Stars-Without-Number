**Shogun** is a sovereign expansionist military power engaged in active territorial conquest across settled space. Unlike criminal organizations such as [[Spacegang]], Shogun is not a pirate network or loose collection of raiders. It maintains organized armed forces, centralized command, established territory, and the industrial capacity necessary to sustain prolonged military operations.

Shogun doctrine emphasizes **rapid deployment, overwhelming force, and coordinated multi-domain warfare**. Its fleets conduct large-scale naval operations while ground forces carry out planetary assaults, station seizures, occupation campaigns, and attacks against strategic infrastructure.

Captured territory is incorporated directly into Shogun's expanding sphere of control. Its forces target transportation routes, industrial assets, communications infrastructure, orbital facilities, and other locations necessary to sustain further expansion. Captured ships, installations, and other useful assets may be incorporated into the Shogun war effort rather than destroyed.

Shogun military formations are disciplined, heavily equipped, and capable of operating as integrated fleets rather than isolated warbands. Known capabilities include capital vessels, planetary assault forces, station-capture units, territorial occupation forces, prisoner transport, and the logistical infrastructure necessary to support extended campaigns.

Relations between Shogun and the [[United Colonies Security Authority]] are openly hostile. Shogun does not recognize U.C.S.A. sovereignty in disputed regions and has attacked U.C.S.A. personnel, vessels, installations, and allied assets without warning. U.C.S.A. security guidance treats confirmed Shogun forces as hostile and advises civilians not to approach Shogun vessels or occupied installations.

Shogun is also known to take prisoners during military operations. Public U.C.S.A. intelligence describes the organization as unwilling to engage in normal diplomatic contact during current hostilities, leaving military confrontation, evacuation, and territorial defense as the primary forms of interaction between the two powers.
