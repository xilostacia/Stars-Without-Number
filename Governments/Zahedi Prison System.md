**Zahedi Prison System** is a major private corrections operator specializing in secure detention infrastructure throughout inhabited space. Governments, corporations, and other authorities contract Zahedi to house, transport, supervise, and employ prisoners in facilities ranging from orbital prisons and holding stations to isolated penal colonies.

Zahedi operates purpose-built correctional facilities intended for long-term containment in locations where conventional prisons would be impractical. Its network includes **47 detention facilities, 12 penal colonies, 8 custodial transport fleets, 23 penal industrial sites, and 11 holding facilities**. Prisoners can be transferred between systems aboard dedicated custodial transports, allowing Zahedi to centralize inmates from multiple jurisdictions within the same correctional network.

A major part of the company's business is **penal industry**. Zahedi facilities use both skilled and unskilled inmate labor for manufacturing, processing, maintenance, resource work, and other contracted production. Remote penal colonies expand this model into isolated regions where inmates can provide labor while serving long-term or high-security sentences.

Zahedi also provides the administrative systems surrounding incarceration, including prisoner intake, classification, transport, facility security, workforce management, and custody records. Its facilities are designed to operate as largely self-contained institutions, particularly at remote sites where outside support may be limited.

One publicly identified facility is **[[Karath Station]]**, an orbital detention facility located in the **[[Karath Belt]]**, specifically identified as operating within **[[Sector 12-4]]**.
