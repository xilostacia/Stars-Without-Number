**Terra Prime Unity** is one of the older heavy-industrial manufacturers still operating across settled space. Originally established on [[Earth]], the company expanded outward with human settlement and eventually shifted the center of its operations away from its homeworld. Its long history has left it with an enormous catalog of standardized designs, industrial systems, and manufacturing infrastructure.

The company manufactures equipment across several major sectors, including **spacecraft, planetary vehicles, industrial machinery, orbital manufacturing systems, and heavy construction equipment**. Its products range from individual components and replacement assemblies to complete ships, specialized vehicles, and large industrial platforms.

Terra Prime vessels are primarily built for civilian, commercial, and industrial use. Rather than focusing on luxury or highly specialized designs, the company emphasizes proven hulls that can be adapted for cargo transportation, industrial work, logistics, or other practical roles. The same approach carries into its planetary vehicle lines, which include heavy transports, extraction vehicles, construction machines, and equipment intended for difficult operating environments.

Its industrial divisions manufacture production machinery, automated systems, fabrication equipment, and large-scale components used throughout manufacturing and resource-processing industries. Terra Prime also operates orbital manufacturing facilities capable of producing structures and machinery too large or impractical for conventional planetary factories.

A major advantage of Terra Prime equipment is compatibility. Generations of designs share standardized components, fittings, and engineering conventions, creating an enormous maintenance and replacement-parts ecosystem. Even equipment that has been in service for decades can often remain operational through refurbishment and replacement rather than complete retirement.

Public company material claims **more than 200 standard product lines**, operations serving **over 50 worlds**, and an industrial history measured in centuries.
