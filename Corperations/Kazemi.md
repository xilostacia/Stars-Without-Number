**Kazemi** is an energy-weapons manufacturer specializing in laser systems and related power technology. Its products range from compact personal weapons to industrial cutting equipment and ship-mounted systems, giving the company a presence in civilian, industrial, security, and aerospace markets.

Personal laser arms form the smallest end of the company's catalog. Kazemi produces compact defensive weapons intended for civilian and professional use alongside larger rifle-pattern systems designed for security, hunting, industrial field work, and other applications where conventional projectile weapons may be less practical.

Industrial and mining lasers are a major part of the company's business. These systems are used for cutting, excavation, resource extraction, construction, demolition, and precision fabrication. Portable industrial units allow crews to perform heavy cutting work in environments where conventional machinery would be difficult to transport or operate.

Kazemi also manufactures large-scale laser systems intended for ships and fixed installations. These include high-output emitters, targeting assemblies, cooling equipment, power-management hardware, and other components needed to integrate directed-energy systems into spacecraft or orbital infrastructure.

Because laser systems are heavily dependent on power supply and thermal management, Kazemi also produces specialized energy packs, capacitors, regulators, cooling assemblies, and related accessories. Many of its weapons and industrial tools are built around interchangeable power modules that can be replaced or serviced independently of the emitter itself.

The company's equipment is commonly found in mining operations, construction sites, research facilities, security installations, and spacecraft. Its technology overlaps with conventional weapons manufacturers such as [[Imani Corporation]] and [[Hsiang Systems]], but Kazemi remains primarily associated with directed-energy technology rather than projectile weapons.
